## Best Home Loans for First Responders in Florida

Most first responders buy with a standard mortgage and then add a program on top. Here's how the main options compare.

| Loan or Program | Down Payment | Credit Score | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FHA | 3.5% | 580+ | Lower credit scores or higher debt |
| Conventional | 3% | 620+ | Good credit; mortgage insurance can be removed later |
| VA | 0% | Set by lender | First responders who served in the military, Guard, or Reserve |
| USDA | 0% | Set by lender | Homes in eligible rural and suburban areas, with income limits |
| Hometown Heroes | Up to $35K | 640+ | First-time buyers working full-time in Florida public safety |
| Good Neighbor Next Door | 50% off | Set by lender | Police, firefighters, and EMTs buying HUD homes |

Many first responders are also veterans, and a VA loan is often the strongest option: no down payment, no monthly mortgage insurance, and flexible credit. Veterans are also exempt from Hometown Heroes' first-time buyer rule. If you served, compare our [Florida VA loan guide](/home-loan/va-loan) first.

## How Lenders Count First Responder Pay

Public safety pay comes from several sources, and lenders treat each one a little differently.

- **Base pay:** Your current salary or hourly rate counts right away, verified with paystubs, W-2s, and a verification of employment from your agency.
- **Overtime and shift differentials:** Lenders usually want about a two-year history, then average it. Twelve to 24 months can work if the income is steady. If your overtime is dropping, they may use the lower recent amount.
- **Off-duty and detail work:** Details paid through your own agency typically show up as part of your pay. Details paid by a separate employer are usually treated as a second job and need a consistent history, often two years.
- **Promotions and step raises:** A documented raise that has already taken effect can count right away.
- **Pensions:** Retirement pay counts when it will continue for at least three years. Bring your award letter.

**Example:** A deputy earning $62,000 a year has about $5,170 a month in base pay. If the deputy has averaged $900 a month in overtime over the last two years, the lender may count about $6,070. At a 45% debt-to-income ratio and a $500 car payment, that leaves room for a housing payment of roughly $2,230 a month, including taxes and insurance.

## Florida Hometown Heroes for First Responders

[Florida Hometown Heroes](/hometown-heroes) is the state's main program for public safety workers. It covers firefighters, certified EMTs, and paramedics at Florida fire departments, ambulance services, and hospitals. It also covers sworn law enforcement officers, correctional and probation officers, juvenile detention officers, and 911 dispatchers at Florida agencies and facilities.

- **Amount:** up to 5% of your first mortgage, with a $35,000 maximum, for down payment and closing costs
- **Terms:** a 0% interest, 30-year second mortgage with no monthly payment. It is not forgiven; you repay it when you sell, refinance, or move out.
- **Requirements:** first-time buyer (veterans are exempt), 640 or higher credit score, full-time employment at a physical Florida location when you close, and [county income limits](/learn/florida-hometown-heroes-income-limits)
- **Funding:** eHousingPlus reported over $20 million available in October 2026. Your lender should confirm availability before you count on it.

Check your role on the full [Hometown Heroes job eligibility list](/learn/florida-hometown-heroes-job-eligibility).

## Good Neighbor Next Door: 50% Off HUD Homes

HUD's [Good Neighbor Next Door program](/learn/florida-good-neighbor-next-door-program) lets full-time law enforcement officers, firefighters, EMTs, and K-12 teachers buy eligible HUD-owned homes at half the list price.

- Homes must be in HUD-designated revitalization areas, and inventory is limited.
- FHA financing is available with as little as $100 down.
- You must live in the home for 36 months.
- When more than one eligible buyer bids, HUD picks by lottery.

The discount is large, but listings are rare. Most first responders treat it as a bonus to watch for rather than their main plan.

## Bank and Credit Union Programs

Some Florida banks and credit unions offer FHLBank Atlanta's Community Partners program, which provides down payment help for current and retired law enforcement officers, firefighters, EMS workers, and other public servants. Amounts vary by institution, and buyers typically need to contribute at least $1,000, complete homebuyer education, and meet income limits.

## Can You Combine Programs?

Often, yes. A common Florida setup is an FHA, VA, or conventional first mortgage plus Hometown Heroes, with seller credits covering part of the closing costs. Some local programs can be layered on top.

Each program has its own rules about which first mortgage it works with, income limits, and how long you must live in the home. Get your options checked before you make an offer, so your contract leaves room for the assistance approvals.

## Homebuying Timeline for First Responders

- **Pre-approval (1–2 weeks):** Review your income, credit, and the best loan and program pairing
- **Homebuyer education (1–3 weeks):** Complete a course if your assistance program requires it
- **House hunting (varies):** Shop within your program's price limits
- **Underwriting and program review (3–6 weeks):** The lender and the assistance program both approve the file
- **Closing (1–2 weeks):** Sign the loan and assistance documents; the assistance applies at closing

A few tips: keep two years of paystubs showing overtime, get a letter from your agency if your schedule changed recently, and budget for Florida homeowners and flood insurance, which can add several hundred dollars a month.
