FHA loans are the most popular mortgage for Florida first-time buyers: 3.5% down with a 580 credit score, flexible income rules, and they pair with every major Florida down payment assistance program. This guide covers everything that determines whether you qualify in 2026 — credit, down payment, county loan limits, mortgage insurance costs, DTI, and the Florida-specific issues (condos, insurance, roofs) that national guides skip.

## FHA Credit Score Requirements in Florida (2026)

Your credit score directly affects approval and down payment requirements. FHA credit requirements are significantly more flexible than conventional loans, which typically require 620 to 680+ scores.

**Minimum FHA Credit Scores**

- **580+ FICO** — Eligible for 3.5% down
- **500 to 579 FICO** — Eligible with 10% down
- **Below 500** — Not eligible under FHA guidelines

Many lenders add their own overlays at 620–640 — if your score is in the 500s, working with a lender that underwrites to FHA's actual minimums matters.

**If Your Credit Score Is Below 580**

You can still qualify by:

- Making a 10% down payment
- Paying down revolving debt
- Avoiding new credit inquiries
- Establishing 12 months of on-time payments

Improving your score can reduce mortgage insurance costs and improve approval odds.

## FHA Down Payment Requirements in Florida

FHA loans offer one of the lowest down payment options available.

| Credit Score | Minimum Down Payment |
|---|---|
| 580+ | 3.5% |
| 500 to 579 | 10% |

**Down Payment Flexibility**

- 100% of the down payment can come from gift funds or a governmental down payment assistance program
- [Florida grants and down payment assistance](/learn/first-time-homebuyer/grants-and-programs) — including [Hometown Heroes](/hometown-heroes) up to $35,000 — layer directly onto FHA loans
- Seller credits toward closing costs allowed (up to 6%)

FHA loans always require mortgage insurance, regardless of down payment size — covered below.

## FHA Loan Limits in Florida (2026)

FHA loan limits cap how much you can borrow and vary by county. For 2026, the one-unit limit is **$541,287** in 51 of Florida's 67 counties. Sixteen counties get higher limits:

| County (1-Unit) | 2026 FHA Limit |
|---|---|
| Most Florida counties (51 of 67) | $541,287 |
| Sarasota, Manatee | $547,400 |
| Duval, St. Johns, Clay, Nassau, Baker (Jacksonville area) | $580,750 |
| Martin, St. Lucie, Okaloosa, Walton | $603,750 |
| Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach | $667,000 |
| Collier (Naples) | $764,750 |
| Monroe (Key West) | $990,150 |

Multi-unit properties (duplex through fourplex) carry higher limits. See the full [Florida FHA loan limits by county](/learn/florida-fha-loan-limits) for every county and unit count.

If a home exceeds your county limit, you can increase your down payment, negotiate the price, or use a [conventional](/learn/conventional-mortgages-in-florida) or jumbo loan instead.

## FHA Mortgage Insurance: What It Costs in 2026

Every FHA loan carries two mortgage insurance premiums (MIP):

- **Upfront MIP: 1.75%** of the loan amount, almost always financed into the loan rather than paid in cash
- **Annual MIP: 0.55%** per year for the typical 30-year loan with less than 5% down (0.50% with 5%+ down), split into your monthly payment

On a $300,000 loan, annual MIP runs about **$137 per month**, plus $5,250 in financed upfront premium.

**How long you pay it:** with the standard 3.5% down payment, annual MIP lasts the life of the loan. If you put 10% or more down, it cancels after 11 years. In practice, most Florida FHA borrowers remove MIP by refinancing into a conventional loan once they reach 20% equity — home appreciation usually gets you there well before year 11.

## FHA Debt-to-Income (DTI) Requirements

DTI measures how much of your income goes toward debt.

**DTI Formula:** Total Monthly Debt / Gross Monthly Income = DTI

**Standard FHA DTI Guidelines**

- Front-end DTI (housing): 31%
- Back-end DTI (total debt): 43%

Treat these as benchmarks, not hard caps. With automated underwriting approval or documented compensating factors — higher credit scores, cash reserves, minimal payment shock, strong income stability — FHA approvals routinely reach **around 50% DTI**, and can go higher.

**A Florida-specific warning:** your homeowners insurance premium counts inside the housing ratio. Florida's high insurance costs eat into how much house your income supports — get an insurance quote early, because it directly changes your approval math.

## FHA Income and Employment Requirements

FHA does not set minimum income limits, but lenders verify stability. Some Florida assistance programs do have [income and purchase price limits](/learn/florida-housing-income-purchase-price-limits) worth checking.

**Typical Documentation**

- Last 30 days of pay stubs
- 2 years of W-2s
- 2 years of tax returns
- Proof of bonuses, overtime, commissions

**Self-Employed Borrowers**

Must provide:

- 2 years of business tax returns
- Profit and Loss statements
- Business license (if applicable)

Non-employment income (alimony, child support, rental income, Social Security and disability benefits) can count if documented and consistent.

**Citizenship note (changed in 2025):** FHA now requires borrowers to be U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents. Non-permanent residents — including DACA recipients and most visa holders — are no longer FHA-eligible. If that affects you, see the [mortgage options still available to DACA homebuyers in Florida](/learn/daca-homebuyers-florida-mortgage-options).

## FHA Home Inspection and Appraisal Requirements

FHA loans require the home to meet minimum property standards.

**FHA Appraisal Focus Areas**

- Structural soundness
- Roof life (2+ years remaining required)
- Electrical, plumbing, HVAC safety
- Water intrusion and moisture
- Lead-based paint hazards
- Termite damage (critical in Florida)

**Inspection vs Appraisal**

- Appraisal: Required by FHA (value + condition)
- Inspection: Strongly recommended (buyer protection)

If the appraisal flags required repairs, they must be completed before closing — or an [FHA 203(k) renovation loan](/learn/fha-203k-loan-florida) can finance them into the mortgage (the Limited 203(k) now covers up to $75,000 in repairs). For the full checklist, see [FHA minimum property standards in Florida](/learn/fha-minimum-property-standards-florida).

## Florida-Specific FHA Issues to Know

**Condos are the hard part.** The building — not just you — must qualify. FHA condo approval requires at least 50% owner-occupancy, no more than 15% of units behind on dues, 10% budget reserves, and adequate insurance. After Florida's structural-inspection and reserve laws tightened, only a small fraction of South Florida buildings remain FHA-eligible. Two workarounds: check HUD's approved-condo list before falling in love with a building, and ask about **Single-Unit Approval**, which can clear an individual unit in an unapproved building.

**Insurance shapes your approval.** Because premiums count in your DTI, Florida's insurance market directly reduces buying power. Quote insurance on a specific home before going under contract.

**Roof rules come from two directions.** FHA requires 2+ years of remaining roof life. Separately, Florida law prevents insurers from refusing coverage solely because a roof is under 15 years old — but older roofs may need an inspection showing 5+ years of useful life to stay insurable. A pre-offer roof check saves Florida FHA deals.

## FHA vs Conventional vs VA Eligibility (Quick Comparison)

| Feature | FHA | Conventional | VA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | Lower down payment and flexible credit | Stronger credit, lower MI with good scores | Eligible veterans and active duty |
| Down payment | Often 3.5% | Can be 3%+ (varies) | Often 0% |
| Credit flexibility | More flexible | Less flexible | Flexible, but lender-dependent |
| Mortgage insurance | FHA MIP required in most cases | PMI if under 20% down, cancellable | No monthly MI (funding fee may apply) |
| Property standards | FHA minimum property standards | Generally less strict | VA appraisal focuses on safety and condition |
| Income limits | None | None | None |

FHA note rates have recently run about 0.4 percentage points below conventional — but MIP narrows the true gap, so compare full monthly payments, not just rates.

## Bottom Line: Do You Qualify for an FHA Loan in Florida?

If you have credit as low as 500, limited savings, higher DTI ratios or non-traditional income, FHA is likely your most accessible path to homeownership in Florida — and it stacks with the state's down payment assistance programs, which can cover most or all of your upfront cost.

Understanding the credit, down payment, MIP, DTI, and property rules upfront puts you in control — and avoids surprises later.
