Rent-to-own lets you move into a Florida home now and buy it later — useful when credit or savings need 1–3 years to catch up. This guide compares every legitimate rent-to-own and lease-purchase option operating in Florida in 2026, with current requirements for each.

> **You may not need rent-to-own.** Most Florida buyers qualify for $10,000–$35,000+ in down payment assistance through grants and forgivable loans. **[Check Your Florida Down Payment Assistance Eligibility](/check-dpa-eligibility)** before committing to a lease-purchase.

## What You'll Typically Need to Qualify

While each rent-to-own program has its own rules, most share a few common requirements. Here's what you can generally expect:

- **Steady income:** Proof of reliable income is usually required to show you can afford monthly payments.
- **Minimum credit score:** Many programs accept credit scores as low as 500–580, but stronger credit can unlock better options.
- **Upfront payment:** Most programs require an initial option fee or deposit, often between 1%–3% of the home's value. [Florida down payment assistance programs](/down-payment-assistance) may help cover this cost.
- **Savings:** Some programs may ask for proof of savings to ensure you're on track for a future mortgage.
- **Clean rental history:** A record of on-time rent payments can boost your approval odds.

Meeting these basics will help you qualify for most rent-to-own opportunities across Florida. For a full breakdown, see our guide to the [requirements to buy a house in Florida](/learn/requirements-to-buy-a-house-in-florida).

## Florida Rent-to-Own & Lease-Purchase Programs

### Landis

**How it works:**
Landis buys a home you choose and rents it to you while you prepare for a mortgage. About 10% of each month's rent is banked toward your future down payment, and you're paired with a homeownership coach throughout the lease.

**Requirements:**

- Credit score around 550 or higher
- Roughly $30,000+ annual household income
- About $3,000 in savings; free application with a soft credit pull

**Best for:** Buyers who want structured coaching and forced savings while rebuilding credit. Available across Florida.

---

### Pathway Homes (Rent+)

**How it works:**
Pathway buys a home from its approved inventory — often newer construction — and leases it to you with a locked option to purchase. If your credit isn't ready, Pathway routes you through credit-building support, and there's no penalty if you walk away at the end of the lease.

**Requirements:**

- Minimum household income of $75,000
- Credit under 600 is routed to credit-building partners first
- Florida markets: Orlando and Tampa

**Best for:** Orlando and Tampa buyers with solid income who want a newer home and price certainty. If your credit score is at least 580, also compare [FHA loan eligibility](/learn/fha-loan-eligibility-requirements-florida) — it requires just 3.5% down.

---

### Trio

**How it works:**
Trio leases you a home for five years with the option to convert to Trio's fixed-rate financing after 18 consecutive on-time payments. Approval focuses on payment history and income rather than credit score alone.

**Requirements:**

- Steady documented income (self-employed and gig income accepted)
- 18 on-time payments to unlock the purchase conversion
- Available in most states — call Trio to confirm your Florida metro

**Best for:** Self-employed and 1099 workers, or buyers recovering from a recent bankruptcy, who need time before qualifying for a [conventional mortgage](/learn/conventional-mortgages-in-florida).

---

### Rent Solutions (Tampa)

**How it works:**
Tampa's "Homeowner in Training" lease-option program. You pay a 3%–5% option-to-buy fee, a fixed portion of each month's rent is credited toward your down payment, and you get a credit-repair runway before buying — from their listings or a home they source for you.

**Requirements:**

- 3%–5% upfront option fee
- Participation in a credit and savings plan
- Tampa Bay area

**Best for:** Tampa renters who want a local, structured path with rent credits.

---

### Alexa Buys Houses (Central Florida)

**How it works:**
A Leesburg-based investor offering lease options on Central Florida homes. You pay market rent plus an option fee that's credited back at closing, and on-time rent payments are reported to credit bureaus to help build your score.

**Requirements:**

- Income and credit review
- Option fee credited at closing
- Central Florida inventory

**Best for:** Central Florida buyers who want their rent payments working toward both the purchase and their credit score.

---

### Florida Future Realty (Cape Coral & Fort Myers)

**How it works:**
A Southwest Florida brokerage that matches buyers with lease-option and seller-financing opportunities in Lee, Charlotte, and Collier counties — typically 1–3 year leases starting with first and last month's rent. This is guided matchmaking rather than a structured program.

**Requirements:**

- Qualification based on income and credit
- Availability depends on current listings

**Best for:** Southwest Florida buyers who want local expert help finding a lease-option deal.

## No-Down-Payment Alternatives to Rent-to-Own

These aren't lease-purchase programs, but they solve the same problem — buying with little or nothing down — and often cost far less than renting to own.

### NACA – Best in America Mortgage & One-Dollar Homes

**How it works:**
NACA's mortgage program offers no down payment, no closing costs, and no minimum credit score — qualification is based on payment history. Through its City One-Dollar Homeownership Program, NACA also partners with cities to sell vacant city-owned homes for $1 plus renovation financing (participation depends on your city — confirm availability with NACA for Florida).

**Requirements:**

- Owner-occupancy required
- NACA counseling and qualification process
- One-Dollar homes require a participating city partnership

**Best for:** Buyers with imperfect credit who can commit to NACA's counseling process. Many affordable target areas overlap with the [cheapest places to buy a house in Florida](/learn/cheapest-places-to-buy-house-in-florida).

---

### NACA – HOT-PHA Program

**How it works:**
Combines Housing Choice Vouchers with NACA's mortgage so low-income renters can buy without a down payment — your voucher's payment standard goes toward the mortgage instead of rent.

**Requirements:**

- Must receive HCV (Section 8) assistance through a participating housing authority
- Participation in NACA's counseling and programs
- Meet income and residency criteria

**Best for:** Voucher recipients ready to transition from renting to owning. If you're close to mortgage-ready, also explore [first-time homebuyer programs](/first-time-home-buyer) that may help you skip the lease entirely.

---

### HUD – Housing Choice Voucher Homeownership Program

**How it works:**
Eligible voucher holders (Section 8) can apply their rental assistance toward homeownership costs like mortgage payments, taxes, and insurance. Participants must complete counseling and work with a participating Public Housing Authority — availability depends on whether your local PHA has opted in.

**Requirements:**

- Must be a current HCV (Section 8) recipient
- First-time homebuyer status
- Meet income, credit, and savings guidelines set by local PHAs

**Best for:** Low-income families seeking long-term stability through existing housing assistance.

---

### USDA – Single Family Housing Programs

**How it works:**
USDA offers true zero-down financing in eligible rural areas of Florida — guaranteed loans through lenders, and direct loans with payment assistance for lower incomes. Much of Florida outside the major metros qualifies.

**Requirements:**

- Home must be in a USDA-eligible rural area
- Income limits by county — roughly $110,000–$130,000 for a 1–4 person household on the guaranteed program
- Must meet creditworthiness and affordability criteria

**Best for:** Buyers open to rural and outer-suburban Florida who want to skip the down payment entirely. Check the [Florida housing income and purchase price limits](/learn/florida-housing-income-purchase-price-limits) to see what you qualify for.

## What Happened to Divvy, Home Partners, and Dream America?

The rent-to-own industry contracted sharply in 2025, and several programs you may have heard of are no longer options in Florida:

- **Divvy Homes** — acquired by Brookfield Properties' Maymont Homes in early 2025. Existing residents kept their leases, but Divvy no longer accepts new rent-to-own applicants.
- **Home Partners of America** — Blackstone wound down operations in February 2025. Existing Right to Purchase agreements are honored by Tricon Residential, but the program is closed to new applicants.
- **Dream America** — pivoted away from rent-to-own to co-living rentals; its rent-to-own application is no longer available.
- **HUD Dollar Homes** — still exists on paper, but it sells only to local governments and qualifying homes (valued under $25,000) essentially no longer exist.

Fewer programs means the ones that remain are more selective — which is why it's worth confirming whether you can simply buy now. Most Florida buyers who think they need rent-to-own actually qualify for an [FHA loan](/learn/fha-loan-eligibility-requirements-florida) with down payment assistance covering most or all of the upfront cost.
